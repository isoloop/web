The examples hub groups the examples in the rail (Basic; Dashboards, with Plant and Markets; Processor, with its Playground and File processor), one panel per page. Basic and the Processor's two pages run in their panel: the app's own idle view, framed, with Start over it; nothing runs until Start, and leaving the panel reloads it idle. "Open full page" above each frame opens the app's own page.
