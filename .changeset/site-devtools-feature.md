`/features/` gains a twelfth feature, **Devtools** (experimental): `connectDevtools` from `@isoloop/devtools` adds a panel in the page with the isolates and their controls, the bus, and a live record log, drawn by an isolate of its own that keeps working through a bus restart and lists itself. It says where memory per isolate is measured, that nothing gates the controls in production, and what the feed costs unread, with a type-checked sample, the devtools guide, and a link to the plant dashboard with the panel open. The landing page's strip lists it, the install block gains `@isoloop/devtools@next`, and the plant and markets tabs on `/examples/` list it with a step to try.
