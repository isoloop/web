With the balancer, a new processor's first 3 s in the quarantine pool, while the balancer measures its load, shows as **Measuring**, and its way out as `measured`, not as an offence. The File processor has a **Restarts in 60 s** control (none, 1, 3, 10), which reloads the page with `?restarts=`; a processor past the limit shows why on its card, with a **Restart** button.
