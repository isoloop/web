# plant

## Earlier releases

Each release is tagged `plant-v<version>` and deployed to isoloop.dev from that tag. Releases up to `plant-v0.2.0` are listed by their [tags](https://github.com/isoloop/web/tags); their history as of that release is added in a later entry ([ADR 0004](../../../docs/adr/0004-publishing-and-governance.md), step 8).
